Pricing FAQ

Why is there a deposit + balance pricing model?

Our two-step pricing ensures quality. The $4.99 deposit confirms your order and initiates data collection. Once we gather and analyze thousands of reviews (which takes 15-30 minutes), you pay the remaining $25 balance to unlock your complete 8,000+ word report. This protects both you and us — you only pay the full price after we've done the work.

What's included in the Free Starter report vs Deep Analysis?

The Free Starter report gives you a market overview, competitive landscape summary, and top 5 customer pain points — perfect for initial research. The Deep Analysis Report ($29.99) expands this to 8,000+ words with detailed competitor breakdowns, comprehensive sentiment analysis across all reviews, actionable marketing copy suggestions, and specific listing optimization recommendations. Think of Free as the trailer, Deep Analysis as the full movie.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes! We offer a 7-day satisfaction guarantee on all paid reports. If the Deep Analysis Report doesn't meet your expectations, contact us within 7 days for a full refund — no questions asked. We're confident in our AI analysis quality, but we want you to feel secure in your purchase.

How does Amz AI Agent compare to Jungle Scout or Helium 10?

Jungle Scout and Helium 10 are subscription-based tools ($49-$229/month) focused on keyword research and sales estimates. Amz AI Agent is a pay-per-report service focused specifically on AI-powered competitor analysis and customer sentiment. We don't replace those tools — we complement them. Use Jungle Scout to find products, then use Amz AI Agent to deeply understand the competitive landscape and customer pain points before you launch.

Can I analyze products from any Amazon marketplace?

Yes! We currently support 8 Amazon marketplaces: United States (amazon.com), Canada (amazon.ca), United Kingdom (amazon.co.uk), Germany (amazon.de), France (amazon.fr), Italy (amazon.it), Spain (amazon.es), and Japan (amazon.co.jp). Simply paste any product ASIN or URL from these marketplaces and our AI will analyze it.

What AI models power the analysis?

We use state-of-the-art large language models including Claude 3.5 Sonnet (for Free reports) and Claude 3.5 Opus (for Deep Analysis reports). These are among the most advanced AI models available, capable of understanding nuanced customer sentiments, identifying patterns across thousands of reviews, and generating actionable business insights that would take humans days to compile.
