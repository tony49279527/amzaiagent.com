Case Study: How an Ordinary "Polishing Wheel" Used AI to Discover Blue Oceans Overlooked by Giants

Introduction: Desperation in the Red Ocean

"Polishing Wheel" is a typical industrial commodity category. Giants abound (like 3M), price wars are brutal, and new products have almost no survival space.

Our user, Seller A, was about to give up on this category. But in one final attempt, he used FlowAI Agent to conduct a full scan of the Listings and Q&A sections of the Top 50 competitors.

1. The "Knowledge Gap" AI Discovered

FlowAgent discovered a high-frequency pattern when analyzing the "User Questions" section:

"Can this handle aluminum rims?"
"What compound should I use for stainless steel?"
"Is this too harsh for chrome?"

Insight: Users don't lack "wheels"—they lack "solutions tailored to specific materials." Current Listings all boast durability, but no one teaches users how to choose or how to use them.

2. Strategy: Become an "Expert" Seller

Based on AI recommendations, Seller A made changes:

  • Product Side: Instead of selling wheels individually, launch "Aluminum Alloy Special Kit" and "Stainless Steel Mirror Kit" bundled with matching polishing compounds.
  • Content Side (Visual): Create a "Material Comparison Chart" image clearly listing which wheel suits which material and what RPM.
  • Traffic Side (Keywords): Abandon the broad keyword "Polishing Wheel" and focus on scenario long-tail keywords like "Aluminum Project Polishing Kit."

3. Results: High-Margin Blue Ocean

While sales volume can't compete with 3M, Seller A's kit is priced at 3x competitor single products, with profit margins reaching 40%. He successfully avoided low-price competition and built his own "professional moat."

In commoditized competition, the "information gap" is the biggest blue ocean. And AI is the fastest shovel for obtaining that information gap.
