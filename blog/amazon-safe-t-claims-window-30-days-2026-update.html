Amazon SAFE-T Claims Window Reduced to 30 Days: Critical 2026 Update for FBA Sellers

Amazon has announced a significant change to its SAFE-T claims process, reducing the filing window from 90 days to just 30 days, effective February 16, 2026. This update impacts all Amazon FBA sellers and requires immediate attention to protect your business interests. As your trusted Amazon FBA consultants, we're here to break down what this means for your operations.

Understanding the New SAFE-T Claims Timeline

The SAFE-T (Seller Appeal for Funds and Errors Transaction) program is crucial for sellers seeking reimbursement for various marketplace issues. This reduction in the claims window represents a 66% decrease in the time sellers have to identify and report discrepancies, making it more critical than ever to maintain vigilant inventory management.

Impact on Amazon FBA Sellers

This policy change creates several challenges for sellers:

  • Increased pressure to monitor inventory discrepancies
  • Need for more frequent reconciliation processes
  • Higher risk of missing legitimate reimbursement opportunities
  • Greater importance of automated monitoring solutions

3 Essential Steps to Adapt to the New Timeline

  • Implement daily inventory reconciliation processes using automated tracking tools
  • Set up automated alerts for inventory discrepancies
  • Create a standardized SAFE-T claims submission workflow

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the new SAFE-T claims window take effect?

The 30-day claims window takes effect on February 16, 2026. All claims after this date must be filed within 30 days of the incident.

What happens if I miss the 30-day window?

Claims filed after the 30-day window will be automatically rejected, with no possibility for appeal. Check our FAQ section for more details on exceptions.

How can I ensure I don't miss claiming opportunities?

Implement automated monitoring systems, conduct daily inventory checks, and maintain detailed records of all transactions and discrepancies.

Conclusion

This significant change to the SAFE-T claims window requires immediate action from Amazon sellers. Stay proactive by implementing the suggested measures and utilizing automated tools to protect your business interests.
